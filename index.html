Live today

Grid monitoring, in the field.

Our pilot system is running on a live distribution transformer, streaming data to our own secure platform. Production hardware is next.

Building next

Grid-forming inverters.

As solar and batteries replace generators, grids lose the inertia that kept them stable. Our grid-forming inverters will act as voltage sources, holding voltage and frequency on weak grids and keeping sites powered through outages.

Why now

On 13 May 2026, a single disturbance at Khavda cost India 8,963 MW of generation. CEA’s July 2026 advisory requires grid-forming inverters for battery projects of 50 MW and above. India needs this technology built at home.

Sources: GRID-INDIA presentation to the National Committee on Transmission, as reported by Renewable Watch; Central Electricity Authority advisory of 10 July 2026, clarified 4 August 2026.
